# Umber App Onboarding Flows

## Option A: Normal Onboarding (Educational)
*Total Time: ~60 seconds*

### Step 1: What's an Umber? (20 seconds)
- **Screen**: "Umbers are your intentional collections - like wishlists, but thoughtful"
- **Visual**: Show example umber ("Dream Home") with a few items in mind map view
- **Copy**: "Each umber helps you organize and reflect on what you want"
- **CTA**: "Create Your First Umber"

### Step 2: Create Your First Umber (30 seconds)
- **Screen**: "What would you like to curate?"
- **Examples**: "Home Renovation," "Wardrobe Refresh," "Gadget Dreams"
- **Input**: Text field for umber name
- **Background**: Auto-generate "Unnested" nest

### Step 3: Add Your First Item (45 seconds)
- **Screen**: "Add something you've been wanting to this umber"
- **Input**: URL paste field
- **Process**: URL scrape → show preview
- **Optional Prompt**: "Want to add any thoughts?" (clearly marked optional)
- **Result**: Item appears in umber

### Step 4: See Your Umber in Action (60 seconds)
- **Screen**: "Here's how your umber looks as a mind map"
- **Visual**: React Flow visualization with their item in Unnested nest
- **Tooltip**: "Items start here, but you can create nests to organize them"
- **CTA**: "Add another item?" button

### Step 5: Dashboard with Soft Setup
- **Persistent Cards**:
  - "Create a nest to organize your items"
  - "Set a budget for this umber"
  - "Try a different theme"

---

## Option B: Most Frictionless (Action-First)
*Total Time: ~45 seconds*

### Step 1: Jump Right In (15 seconds)
- **Screen**: "Create your first Umber"
- **Examples**: "Home Wishlist," "Tech Dreams," "Style Goals" (underneath)
- **Subtle Copy**: "Umbers help you organize your wants thoughtfully"
- **No Philosophy**: Just gentle positioning
- **Input**: Text field for umber name

### Step 2: Add First Item (30 seconds)
- **Screen**: "Add something you want"
- **Input**: URL paste field
- **Process**: URL scrape → show preview
- **Optional Prompt**: "Any thoughts?" box (very casual)
- **Result**: Item appears immediately

### Step 3: Visual Reveal (45 seconds)
- **Automatic Transition**: To mind map view
- **Screen**: "Here's your Umber visualized"
- **Visual**: Their item in "Unnested" area
- **One-line Explainer**: "Add more items or create nests to organize them"
- **CTA**: Floating add button

### Ongoing: Dashboard Discovery
- **Soft Setup Cards** appear as needed:
  - "Create a nest to organize your items"
  - "Set a budget for this umber"
  - "Try a different theme"

---

## Key Differences

| Aspect | Normal | Most Frictionless |
|--------|--------|-------------------|
| **Time** | 60 seconds | 45 seconds |
| **Explanation** | Upfront definition | Learn through doing |
| **Philosophy** | Explicit contemplative messaging | Discover naturally |
| **Mind Map** | Explained step | Surprise reveal |
| **Friction** | 4 deliberate steps | 3 action steps |
| **User State** | Informed and ready | Curious and engaged |

---

## Recommendation
**Go with Most Frictionless** - the mind map visualization is the real "aha moment" that differentiates Umber. Let that do the explaining rather than text. Users who want depth will discover the contemplative features naturally through usage.
